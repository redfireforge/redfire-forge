1. Purpose & Use Case

The Trash Box is RedfireForge's safety net for accidental deletions. Every time you delete a Feature Group, Scenario, Test, or Shared Data Source, it is moved to the Trash Box instead of being permanently removed.

This manual walks you through:

  • Deleting a scenario and using instant undo to recover it immediately.
  • Opening the Trash Panel to browse and search deleted items.
  • Restoring items from the Trash Panel back to their original location.
  • Permanently deleting items and emptying the trash.
  • Configuring the retention period and max items settings.

2. Concepts You'll Learn

  • Soft delete — items are preserved in storage for a configurable retention period instead of immediate permanent removal.
  • Undo toast — a 5-second notification with an Undo button for instant recovery after any delete.
  • Trash Panel — a modal for browsing, searching, restoring, and permanently deleting trashed items.
  • Automatic purge — expired items are cleaned up on app startup.
  • ID collision handling — restored items get new IDs when a conflict exists.
  • Orphan recovery — items whose parent was deleted are restored into a "Restored Items" group.

4. Trash Box Overview

Feature Description
Undo Toast Appears for 5 seconds after any delete. Click Undo to instantly restore.
Trash Panel Modal opened via the Trash button in the toolbar. Browse, search, restore, or permanently delete items.
Retention Period Configurable: 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days. Default is 30 days.
Max Items Configurable: 50, 100, or 200. When exceeded, oldest expired items are removed first.
Supported Types Feature Groups, Scenarios, Tests, and Shared Data Sources.

5. Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1 — Import the Sample

  1. Go to Gallery → Tests.
  2. Find Trash Recovery Demo in the list.
  3. Click Import to add it to your harness.
  4. You should see a Feature Group named Trash Box — Recovery Demo with two scenarios.

Step 2 — Delete a Scenario

  1. In the Harness sidebar, expand Trash Box — Recovery Demo.
  2. Right-click on Sample Scenario and select Delete.
  3. A confirmation dialog appears: "Move scenario ‘Sample Scenario’ to Trash?"
  4. Click Move to Trash to confirm.
  5. The scenario disappears from the sidebar.
💡 Tip Notice the undo toast that appears at the bottom of the screen. You have 5 seconds to click Undo and instantly restore the scenario.

Step 3 — Use Instant Undo

  1. Observe the undo toast: "Sample Scenario moved to Trash"
  2. Click the Undo button before the progress bar expires.
  3. The scenario reappears in the sidebar exactly where it was.

Step 4 — Delete Again (Let It Go This Time)

  1. Delete Sample Scenario again.
  2. This time, wait 5 seconds or click the ✕ dismiss button on the toast.
  3. The item is now in the Trash Box.

Step 5 — Open the Trash Panel

  1. Click the Trash button in the toolbar (top-right). A badge shows the item count.
  2. The Trash Panel opens showing your deleted scenario.
  3. You can see: entity name, parent path, deletion time, expiry countdown, and entity type.

Step 6 — Restore from Trash

  1. Find Sample Scenario in the Trash Panel.
  2. Click the Restore button.
  3. The scenario reappears in the Trash Box — Recovery Demo Feature Group.
  4. The item disappears from the Trash Panel.

Step 7 — Permanently Delete an Item

  1. Delete Another Scenario and wait for the undo toast to expire.
  2. Open the Trash Panel.
  3. Click the Delete button next to the item.
  4. A confirmation dialog appears: "Permanently delete ‘Another Scenario’? This cannot be undone."
  5. Click Delete to confirm. The item is gone forever.
⚠ Warning Permanent deletion cannot be undone. The item's data is removed from storage entirely.

Step 8 — Configure Retention Settings

  1. Open the Trash Panel.
  2. In the footer, find the Retention dropdown and change it to 7 days.
  3. Find the Max items dropdown and change it to 50.
  4. Settings are saved automatically and apply to all future deletions.

6. Try It Yourself

Now that you've learned the Trash Box workflow, try these exercises:

Exercise 1 — Delete and Restore a Feature Group

Delete the entire Trash Box — Recovery Demo Feature Group. Open the Trash Panel and restore it. Notice that both scenarios and their tests are preserved.

Exercise 2 — Delete a Test

Expand a scenario, delete a single test, and restore it from the Trash Panel. Notice the parent path shows the Feature Group and Scenario it came from.

Exercise 3 — Search in Trash

Delete multiple items, then use the Trash Panel search bar to find a specific one by name.

Exercise 4 — Empty Trash

Delete a few items, open the Trash Panel, and click Empty Trash. Confirm the action and verify the trash is now empty.

📋 Note All exercises modify your local data. You can always re-import the gallery sample to start fresh.
